Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: January 2026

Scope of this policyScope of this policy

This privacy policy applies to the ROI Loan Experts website and to the information we collect when you submit an enquiry, eligibility check, request a document check, communicate with us, or otherwise share information with us. ROI Loan Experts is a loan assistance and DSA service and does not lend money directly.

Information we collectInformation we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact and identity information such as your name, mobile number, email address and city.
  • Enquiry information such as the loan type you are interested in, the amount required, your employment type and approximate income.
  • Documents you choose to share such as KYC documents, income proof, bank statements, business records and property documents.
  • Technical information such as your IP address, device type, browser type and pages visited, collected through ordinary website logs.
  • Communications such as call notes, messages and documents exchanged with our team or a lender, where you have consented.

Please do not submit OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVV codes, net banking credentials or card details at any time. We never ask for them.

How we use your informationHow we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the loan assistance service you requested.
  • To review documents and inform you, in preliminary terms, about potentially suitable options.
  • To share relevant information with a bank or financial institution only where you have instructed or consented to it.
  • To maintain records of our interaction for service, compliance and dispute-resolution purposes.
  • To improve our website, services and communication.
  • To comply with applicable law, regulatory requirements and legitimate internal policies.

Legal basis for processingLegal basis for processing

We process your information on the basis of your consent, the performance of a service you have requested, our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and improving our services, and compliance with applicable legal obligations. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us.

Sharing your informationSharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with:

  • Banks, financial institutions, NBFCs and their empanelled agents, where required to explore or process a loan option for which you have consented.
  • Service providers who support hosting, communication, document handling or analytics, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities or regulators, where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

A lender receiving your information conducts its own verification and privacy process under its own policies.

Storage, retention and securityStorage, retention and security

We retain enquiry and service records for as long as reasonably necessary to serve your requirement, meet legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary by record type. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Cookies and similar technologiesCookies and similar technologies

This website uses only essential storage required to operate the site. If analytics or advertising cookies are introduced in future, this policy and any consent mechanism will be updated before they are used.

Links to other websitesLinks to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites. Once you leave our website, your information is governed by the privacy policy of that third party. We are not responsible for their content or practices.

Your rightsYour rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion of information, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • Withdrawal of consent for future processing.
  • Information about the parties with whom we have shared your information.

To make a request, contact us using the details on the contact page. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Consent and acceptanceConsent and acceptance

By submitting an enquiry or eligibility form on this website, you confirm that the information you have shared is accurate to the best of your knowledge, that you consent to its use as described in this policy, and that you have read our Disclaimer and Terms & Conditions.

Changes to this policyChanges to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable requirements. Material changes will be indicated on this page. Your continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.

ContactContact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or your information, please contact us through the contact page. Our contact details will be published there once confirmed.

Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Proprietorships & partnerships

  • Registered business with an operational track record as required by the lender
  • Business registration and KYC documents
  • Bank statements and financial statements or ITR
  • Address and business premises proof, if applicable

Companies, LLPs & professionals

  • Constitutional or registration documents for the entity
  • Audited or CA-certified financials as required
  • GST or tax records where applicable
  • Credit assessment of the entity and its promoters
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

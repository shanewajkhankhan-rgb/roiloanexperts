In detail

What Happens at Each Stage

Knowing the details in advance removes most of the uncertainty from a loan journey.

  • No upfront commitment
  • You can stop at any stage
  • Lender terms explained before proceeding
  • No guaranteed approval claims
Start With Eligibility

  1. Enquiry

    You submit your requirement through the eligibility form, contact page or a direct conversation with our team.

  2. Preliminary profile assessment

    We consider income source, existing obligations, purpose and documentation readiness to understand which categories may be relevant.

  3. Document collection and verification

    You share the relevant KYC, income and supporting documents. We guide you if a document is unclear, expired or missing.

  4. Option identification

    We identify options available across our banking and financial partners, along with the indicative requirements of each.

  5. Application and lender submission

    With your consent, the application is submitted to the lender through its process. Fees, if any, are informed beforehand.

  6. Lender verification and decision

    The lender conducts its own verification and credit assessment and communicates its decision, amount, rate and terms.

  7. Sanction, documentation and disbursal

    On satisfaction, the lender completes documentation, agreement and disbursal as per its process.

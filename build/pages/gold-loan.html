Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Ownership & KYC

  • Clear ownership of the gold being offered as security
  • KYC and address proof of the applicant as required by the lender
  • Any documents requested by the lender for ownership or purity
  • No undisclosed disputes over the assets offered

Valuation considerations

  • Valuation is normally carried out by a valuer appointed by the lender
  • Purity, weight and the current market rate influence the valuation
  • Terms including rate, tenure and processing vary by lender and profile
  • The amount offered is based on valuation, not a guaranteed percentage of gold value
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

FAQ

Gold Loan Questions

Eligibility of jewellery, coins or bars and the accepted purity levels are defined by each lender.

A valuer appointed by the lender checks purity and weight and applies the applicable market rate as per the lender’s policy.

The amount is based on the valuation and the lender’s loan-to-valuation policy. No percentage can be guaranteed in advance.

Assets pledged to a lender remain under its custody as per the agreement. The terms are set out in the lender’s contract.

Foreclosure and top-up options depend on the lender’s policy and the terms in your agreement.

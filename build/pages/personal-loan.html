Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Salaried applicants

  • Income from a recognised employer, with payslips or salary account statements
  • KYC documents as required by the lender
  • Existing EMIs and obligations usually considered during assessment
  • Age, credit profile and employer category are decided by the lender

Self-employed & professionals

  • Business income with proof such as ITR, computation of income or bank statements
  • KYC and business registration documents where applicable
  • Continuity of business and filing regularity
  • Credit assessment and verification by the lender
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

What Lenders Usually Examine

Credit profile and existing EMIs are usually among the first things a lender evaluates.

Income stability

Regularity and continuity of income generally support a stronger profile.

Existing obligations

Total monthly obligations are compared against income.

Documentation quality

Clear, complete and legible documents reduce verification friction.

Employer or business category

Some lenders treat employers or business types differently.

Requested amount

The amount should be reasonable in relation to profile and purpose.

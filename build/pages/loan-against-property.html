Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Owner-borrower considerations

  • Clear and marketable title to the property offered as security
  • Property free from major disputes or encumbrances, as assessed by the lender
  • Income and credit profile supporting the proposed EMI
  • KYC and address proof of the applicant and co-applicants

Property considerations

  • Residential, commercial or mixed-use property, as accepted by the lender
  • Property should generally be in an accepted location and condition
  • Valuation is carried out by the lender or its empanelled valuer
  • Documents typically include title deed, tax receipts and approved plans
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

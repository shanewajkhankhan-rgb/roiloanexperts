Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Salaried individuals

  • Regular income with payslips or salary statements
  • KYC and address proof
  • Proforma invoice or quotation and insurance details
  • Credit assessment and existing EMIs are evaluated by the lender

Self-employed & commercial operators

  • Business income proof such as ITR and bank statements
  • Business KYC and registration documents
  • Permit, fitness and registration documents for commercial vehicles
  • Route or permit documentation where applicable
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

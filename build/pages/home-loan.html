Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Salaried applicants

  • Regular income with payslips or salary account statements
  • KYC and address proof
  • Property documents, title and approved plan where applicable
  • Credit history and existing EMIs are assessed by the lender

Self-employed & business owners

  • Consistent business or professional income with ITR and bank statements
  • Business KYC and registration documents
  • Property documents including title chain and clearances
  • Income assessment for such profiles is usually more detailed
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

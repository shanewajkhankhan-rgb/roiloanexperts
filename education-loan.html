Eligibility

Who May Be Eligible

Indicative only. Final eligibility is determined by the respective lender after verification.

Student & co-applicant documents

  • Admission letter or offer from a recognised institution
  • Academic mark sheets and previous qualification certificates
  • Identity and address proof of the student
  • Identity, income and address proof of the co-applicant, if required

Indicative eligibility considerations

  • Course and institution acceptance vary by lender
  • Age criteria for the applicant and co-applicant are set by the lender
  • A co-applicant with a verifiable income source is usually needed
  • Repayment is generally structured around the expected income after the course
Please note: Meeting the above conditions does not guarantee eligibility or approval. Lenders apply their own criteria, credit assessment and internal policies.

Core documents

The Common Checklist

Most loan categories start with these. Keep clear, legible scans or photocopies ready.

PAN CardPrimary identity document. Usually mandatory across lenders.
Aadhaar / KYCAddress and identity verification, or an accepted alternative.
Salary SlipsRecent payslips, generally for salaried applicants.
Bank StatementsSalary or business account statements for the required period.
ITR / Income Tax ReturnOften required for self-employed and business applicants.
Business DocumentsRegistration, GST and business proof where applicable.
Address ProofUtility bill, rent agreement or other acceptable proof.
Employment ProofOffer letter, appointment letter or employment ID.
Property DocumentsTitle, agreement and valuation papers where applicable.
Important: Document review does not guarantee loan approval. Final eligibility and approval are subject to the respective lender’s policies, verification and underwriting.

By applicant type

What to Prepare Based on Your Profile

Indicative groupings. The lender may ask for more or fewer documents depending on the product and your profile.

Salaried

  • PAN and Aadhaar or accepted KYC
  • Recent salary slips
  • Salary account bank statements
  • Employment ID or offer letter
  • Address proof, if required

Self-employed

  • PAN and Aadhaar or accepted KYC
  • ITR for the relevant years
  • Bank statements of the business account
  • Computation of income or CA certificate
  • Business address proof

Business owners

  • Business registration documents
  • GST registration, if applicable
  • Financial statements or ITR
  • Business bank statements
  • KYC of directors or partners

Category specific

Additional Documents By Loan Type

Home Loan & Loan Against Property

  • Sale agreement or title deed
  • Chain of title documents
  • Encumbrance certificate
  • Approved building plan
  • Property tax receipts and possession proof

Vehicle Loan

  • Proforma invoice or purchase quotation
  • Insurance details for the vehicle
  • Registration certificate, for used vehicles
  • Permit and fitness certificate, for commercial vehicles
  • Existing loan or hypothecation details, if any

Education Loan

  • Admission letter or offer from the institution
  • Academic mark sheets and certificates
  • Detailed fee structure from the institution
  • Student KYC and co-applicant KYC
  • Co-applicant income proof

Gold Loan

  • KYC and address proof
  • Gold assets offered as security
  • Purity or hallmark report, where applicable
  • Ownership or purchase proof, if requested
  • Income proof, if required for the amount sought

Before you share

Simple Preparation Tips

  • Scan documents clearly with all four corners visible.
  • Make sure names and dates match across documents.
  • Keep original documents safe; share copies unless asked otherwise.
  • Check expiry dates on older documents such as older PAN cards or visas.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or net banking credentials with anyone.
  • Ask us if you are unsure whether a document applies to your case.
